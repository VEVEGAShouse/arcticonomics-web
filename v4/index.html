01 — The law

The Arctic economy runs counter‑cyclical to the world.

In a world of trust and free trade, the cheapest barrel wins. The Arctic is cold, remote and costly, so nobody needs it. When the world breaks, it becomes vital, whatever the cost. Move the world and see.

    06 — The great Nordic paradox

    The Nordics win only when they are the exception.

    Touch each word

    If the Nordics made the whole world as peaceful as they are, they would erase the very conditions of their success. That is not a scandal. It is a story that no longer matches the facts, and the world is starting to notice.
