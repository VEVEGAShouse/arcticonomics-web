Now, the Arctic

Far. Cold. Costly.

In a united world, the Arctic is worth nothing.

Everyone trusts each other and trades freely. The cheapest wins. Why go to the hardest place on Earth?

When the world breaks, the Arctic becomes vital.

Mistrust, blockades, sanctions, war. Supplies disappear, and people pay whatever it takes.

The law of Arcticonomics

The Arctic moves opposite to the world.

Greenland has not changed.
The world around it has.

More divided, more nationalist, less trust. Now everyone wants it as a partner.

The great Nordic paradox

Global peace or Nordic wealth. Pick one.

With global peace, cheap oil returns and Norway’s wealth collapses. Nobody buys Swedish weapons.

The Nordics win only when they are the exception.

  • Democratic in a world that turns autocratic.
  • Stable in a world that turns chaotic.
  • United in a world that turns fragmented.

The real risk

Not a scandal. A credibility crisis.

Say ≠ do→Confusion→Mistrust→Credibility lost

The Nordics sell themselves as the world’s moral superpowers. The world is starting to notice the gap.

Time to rethink the idea of the Nordics.

Like Don Quijote, the mistake is not in dreaming. It is in refusing to let the dream evolve.
